Main horse and show hubs

  • Winnipeg east and Birds Hill: Oakbank, Sunnyside, Cooks Creek, Anola, East St. Paul, Birds Hill Provincial Park and MHC Equestrian Centre, Red River Exhibition Park, Assiniboia Downs
  • Winnipeg south and west: Headingley, Grande Pointe, Sanford, Oak Bluff, St. Norbert, Navin
  • Brandon and Westman: Keystone Centre and Royal Manitoba Winter Fair, Thunderbird Horse Center, Carroll and Manitoba Horse Trials

Regional, Highway 1 and south corridors

  • Selkirk and St. Andrews: Lockport, Clandeboye, Stonewall, Stony Mountain
  • Steinbach and southeast: La Broquerie, Grunthal, Richer, Ste. Anne
  • Morris and the Red River Valley: Manitoba Stampede grounds, Rosenort, Niverville
  • Portage la Prairie and Poplar Point

Agricultural and rodeo corridors

  • Morden and Winkler: Pembina Valley, Osterwick
  • Virden and Elkhorn (toward Saskatchewan)
  • Dauphin and Riding Mountain: Ste. Rose du Lac, Neepawa, Minnedosa
  • Interlake: Ashern, Lundar, Teulon, Arborg
