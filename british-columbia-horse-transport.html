Lower Mainland and BC Interior journeys

For trips involving Langley, the Vancouver area, Kamloops, Kelowna and nearby communities, include the exact barn addresses in your request. We can consider points away from a main corridor when the schedule and route make sense.

Mountain routes need thoughtful scheduling

Crossing the Rockies or travelling through the BC Interior can involve changing weather, road conditions and longer driving days. We plan around the actual route and conditions rather than promising fixed travel times before the details are reviewed.

Trips in either direction

We accept enquiries for Alberta-to-BC and BC-to-Alberta transport. A return-route opportunity may make a trip possible even when you are not near the beginning of a scheduled run. Tell us your date flexibility and preferred pickup window.

Preparing for transport

Please provide horse count, size, loading experience, veterinary or handling considerations and trailer access details. The booking form is a request for an estimate—not an automatically confirmed reservation.
