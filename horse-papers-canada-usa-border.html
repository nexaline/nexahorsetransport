What papers does a horse need to cross the Canada–US border?

Health certificates, Coggins tests and customs, both directions.

Short answer: a horse going from Canada into the US needs an official veterinary health certificate endorsed by the Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA), and a negative Equine Infectious Anemia (EIA or "Coggins") test taken within 180 days. Coming into Canada from the US, a horse needs a USDA-endorsed health certificate and a current EIA test. Your vet starts the process.

Canada to the USA

  • Health certificate: from your veterinarian, issued within 30 days of travel and endorsed by a CFIA veterinarian.
  • EIA (Coggins) test: negative, with blood drawn within 180 days before the horse enters the US, at an approved lab.
  • Time in Canada: the certificate states the horse has lived in Canada for the last 60 days. Horses here for less than 60 days need extra papers.
  • Temporary or permanent: horses going for less than 30 days (shows, clinics) are a temporary entry. Horses being sold or moving for good are a permanent entry, need a USDA vet inspection at the border, and the border port must be booked ahead.
  • US state rules: some US states ask for their own permit or entry number. Check the state you are going to.

USA to Canada

  • Health certificate: a US export health certificate for Canada (forms VS 17-140 or VS 17-145), issued by a USDA-accredited vet and endorsed by USDA.
  • EIA (Coggins) test: current and negative.
  • Special cases: horses from some US states with disease outbreaks need a CFIA import permit first. We check this before every trip.

Canadian horses coming home

A Canadian horse coming back from the US should travel with its proof of export from Canada and the right health papers. If the horse returns to the same owner with proof of export, it is treated as Canadian goods returning and no GST is charged on it. Keep every paper from the trip down.

Customs broker

A customs broker files the border paperwork for sales and permanent moves. We can work with your broker, or help you find one. Broker, vet and government fees are passed on at cost.

Good to know

Rules change, especially during disease outbreaks. Always confirm with your vet, the CFIA and USDA APHIS before the trip. This guide is general information, not legal or veterinary advice.

Quick answers

What is a Coggins test?

A blood test for Equine Infectious Anemia (EIA). To enter the US from Canada, the test must be negative and taken within 180 days before entry.

Does my horse need a permit to go from Canada to the US?

Most horses that have lived in Canada for at least 60 days do not need a USDA import permit for road travel. They need a CFIA-endorsed health certificate and a negative EIA test.

How long is the health certificate good for?

For horses going from Canada to the US, the health certificate is from an exam within 30 days of travel.
