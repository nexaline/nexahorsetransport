Long-distance horse transport across Canada

Moving a horse between provinces takes more than choosing the shortest road. Nexa Horse Transport helps plan cross-country travel with attention to the horse’s comfort, suitable stops and communication with the owner.

Request a horse transport estimate

Multi-province planning

Our published route includes major Canadian corridors with opportunities to discuss pickup and delivery in horse communities along the way. Availability depends on the actual run, horse space and whether the additional travel can be accommodated.

Horse welfare on longer journeys

A longer journey may require more detailed planning for rest, water, feed and monitoring. Tell us about health, handling and travel needs before committing to a date. We will discuss arrangements appropriate to the route and the horse.

Shows, purchases and relocations

Transport enquiries may involve moving between barns, delivering a recently purchased horse, or arranging travel to an event. The receiving party’s details, loading access and arrival constraints help us work out a practical plan.

How to request your estimate

Enter pickup and drop-off points, horses and preferred dates using the website’s estimate form. The displayed amount is an estimate only. We review the request and confirm space, timing and final arrangements separately.
