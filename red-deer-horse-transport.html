Where we haul around Red Deer

Red Deer sits halfway between Calgary and Edmonton, in the middle of Alberta's horse country. We haul barn to barn across central Alberta, with no meeting in parking lots.

Quick answers

How much does horse transport cost in Red Deer?

Nexa charges per horse, per kilometre, plus GST. Trips under 400 km are $2.00 per km for a standard stall, with a $300 minimum per trip. Red Deer to Calgary or Edmonton (about 150 km) is about $300 + GST for one horse.

Which areas around Red Deer do you serve?

Red Deer, Sylvan Lake, Lacombe, Blackfalds, Ponoka, Innisfail, Penhold, Olds, Sundre, Rocky Mountain House, Stettler and the farms in between.

Can you haul from Red Deer to BC?

Yes. Red Deer to Langley, BC is about 1,100 km, about $1,320 + GST for one horse in a standard stall.
