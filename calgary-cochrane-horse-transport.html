Horse transport in Calgary & Cochrane

Nexa Horse Transport is based in Cochrane, just west of Calgary. We offer horse transportation barn to barn around the Calgary region: to a new barn, a show, a trainer or the vet, with the same careful loading on every trip.

Where we haul around Calgary

Our truck and trailer live in Cochrane, so trips anywhere in the Calgary region start close to home. We go barn to barn, with no meeting in parking lots.

West and Foothills

  • Cochrane: our home base, acreages and boarding barns along Highways 1A and 22
  • Bragg Creek, Springbank and Priddis: trail barns and private acreages

Calgary and south

  • Calgary: Spruce Meadows, training barns and equine clinics around the city
  • De Winton, Okotoks, High River and Millarville: show barns and breeding farms

North and east

  • Airdrie, Crossfield, Carstairs, Didsbury and Olds: along Highway 2
  • Sundre: west of Olds, with lots of horse farms

Quick answers

How much does horse transport cost in Calgary?

Nexa charges per horse, per kilometre, plus GST. Trips under 400 km are $2.00 per km for a standard stall, with a $300 minimum per trip. Calgary to Edmonton (about 300 km) is about $600 + GST for one horse.

Where is Nexa Horse Transport based?

In Cochrane, Alberta, just west of Calgary. Our truck and trailer are kept there.

Do you haul horses to Spruce Meadows?

Yes. We haul to and from Spruce Meadows and other show grounds, barns and clinics around Calgary.

How much notice do I get before pickup?

At least two hours before pickup and before delivery.
