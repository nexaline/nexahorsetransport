Horse transport between Alberta and Ontario

Moving a horse from Calgary or Edmonton toward Ontario, or bringing a horse west? Nexa Horse Transport accepts enquiries for long-distance, multi-province journeys in either direction. Each request is reviewed for route fit, suitable space and timing.

Get a horse transport estimate

Eastbound and westbound transport

For journeys between Alberta and Ontario, the practical pickup and delivery points matter more than a city name alone. Include the barn addresses and preferred date windows. Tell us whether you have flexibility to meet a planned trip or need a specific pickup day; we will review the request before confirming availability.

Ontario, Prairie and Alberta route planning

Our published coast-to-coast route includes Ontario and the Prairie provinces. Canadian pickup and delivery journeys need a route that stays within Canada. Distance estimates are preliminary and can change with road conditions, weather, pickup access and any requested route changes.

Travel arrangements for each horse

Let us know the number and size of horses, how they load and travel, and whether any need different stall arrangements. Tell us about any recent veterinary concerns or handling needs before booking. Details like these help us assess whether our trailer and schedule are a suitable match.

Why request an estimate before choosing a carrier?

Compare the whole journey, not just the advertised price: pickup and delivery windows, stall type, planned stops, horse handling, communication, and what is included in the quote. With Nexa you can submit both addresses and horse details online, then discuss the request with our team. An online estimate is not a confirmed booking.
