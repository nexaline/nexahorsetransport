How much does it cost to ship a horse from Alberta to Ontario?

Prices per horse, worked examples and what's included.

Short answer: with Nexa Horse Transport, one horse in a standard stall from Calgary to Toronto costs about $4,080 plus GST (about $4,284). The trip is about 3,400 km on Canadian highways, and long-distance trips are priced at $1.20 per horse, per kilometre for a standard stall.

How the price is worked out

Our prices are in Canadian dollars, per horse, per kilometre, plus GST. There is no booking fee and no fuel surcharge. Trips over 400 km use our long-distance rates:

  • Standard stall: $1.20 per km
  • Roomier stall: $1.40 per km
  • Box stall: $2.00 per km (good for mares with foals, very large horses or horses that travel better loose)

Vet papers and any government fees are charged at cost. The minimum charge is $300 per trip.

Example prices: Calgary to Toronto (about 3,400 km)

  • Standard stall: 3,400 km × $1.20 = $4,080 + GST
  • Roomier stall: 3,400 km × $1.40 = $4,760 + GST
  • Box stall: 3,400 km × $2.00 = $6,800 + GST

Two horses in standard stalls cost twice the one-horse price. Edmonton, Red Deer or Sundre to the Ottawa area is a longer trip, so the price goes up with the kilometres. Your exact distance comes from the addresses you enter in our estimate form.

The route stays in Canada

Trips between Alberta and Ontario stay on Canadian highways, through Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Thunder Bay, Sault Ste. Marie and Sudbury. No border crossing means no US health papers and no border delays. See our Alberta to Ontario horse transport page for the full route.

How long does it take?

Plan on about four to five days on the road, with rest stops and fresh water around the clock. Weather on the north shore of Lake Superior can add time in winter. You get at least two hours' notice before pickup and before delivery.

Papers to have ready

Horses leaving Alberta need an Alberta livestock inspection or a horse permit from Livestock Identification Services. Some barns and events in Ontario also ask for a current Coggins (EIA) test. Ask us if you are not sure what your horse needs.

Quick answers

How much does it cost to ship a horse from Calgary to Toronto?

About $4,080 plus GST for one horse in a standard stall with Nexa Horse Transport (about 3,400 km at $1.20 per km). A roomier stall is about $4,760 and a box stall about $6,800, plus GST.

Are there extra fees?

No booking fee and no fuel surcharge. Vet, government and permit fees are charged at cost. Extra costs can apply for long delays at pickup or delivery, extra stops or layovers.

Does the trip go through the United States?

No. Our Alberta to Ontario trips stay on Canadian highways the whole way.
