Horse transport between British Columbia and Ontario

Moving a horse from BC to Ontario or bringing one back west? Nexa accepts enquiries for long-distance journeys between British Columbia and Ontario. Tell us where your horse needs to travel and when; our team will review route fit and available transport options.

Request a horse transport estimate

British Columbia pickup and delivery

For BC journeys, useful locations to include are Langley, the Vancouver area, Kamloops, Kelowna and nearby horse communities. Exact barn access and travel conditions affect the route, so please provide full pickup or delivery details.

Ontario pickup and delivery

Ontario enquiries may involve Toronto-area barns, Ottawa, Sudbury or other communities. Include the exact location instead of just the province so we can assess distance and practical routing.

Eastbound and westbound across the Prairies

Journeys between BC and Ontario can pass through Alberta, Saskatchewan and Manitoba. We review mountain road conditions, distance, practical stop planning and horse comfort before confirming a journey. We do not promise a fixed departure until a booking is accepted.

What to include in your transport request

Provide the number of horses, approximate size, loading behaviour, preferred dates, any special requirements and both barn addresses. An online request is an estimate enquiry, not a confirmed seat or stall.
