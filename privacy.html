Privacy Policy

Nexa Horse Transport is a brand of NEXA LINE LTD., an Alberta corporation.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexa Horse Transport ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

1. Information We Collect

When you request a quote, book a trip, or contact us, we may collect:

  • your name, phone number and email address;
  • pickup and delivery locations, including addresses and GPS coordinates;
  • preferred travel dates;
  • details about your horse(s), such as number, stall type, breed, age, size, temperament, and health or handling needs;
  • messages you send us, and records of calls and text messages with us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • to prepare quotes and confirm bookings;
  • to plan your horse's journey, care for them on the way, and send you trip updates;
  • to provide customer support and answer your questions;
  • to meet legal, tax, and border and animal-health requirements, such as health certificates and customs documents.

3. Text Messaging (SMS)

You can choose to receive text messages from Nexa Horse Transport about your quote, booking confirmations, and pickup and delivery updates.

  • How you opt in: by ticking the text message box on our booking form (it is unchecked by default), or by agreeing verbally when you speak with us by phone.
  • Optional: agreeing to receive text messages is optional and is not a condition of booking.
  • How often: message frequency varies.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opting out: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
  • Contact: call or text us at 1-844-491-6392.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

4. How We Share Information

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed to provide our services, and only with:

  • service providers who help us run our business, such as our phone and text messaging provider (Twilio), email, mapping and accounting services;
  • veterinarians, when needed for your horse's health or travel requirements;
  • customs brokers, for trips that cross the Canada–US border;
  • government agencies, such as the Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA) and the US Department of Agriculture (USDA), when required for transport or border crossing;
  • others, when required by law.

Some of our service providers may store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

5. Security and Retention

We use reasonable physical, organisational and technical safeguards to protect personal information against loss, theft and unauthorised access. We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law (for example, for tax records), and then securely delete or destroy it.

6. Your Rights

You may ask to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw your consent, including consent to receive text messages (reply STOP at any time).

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on your request.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us:

  • Phone: 1-844-491-6392
  • Email: nexahorsetransport@gmail.com

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page, with the date it was last updated.
