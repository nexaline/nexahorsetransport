Coast-to-coast horse transport across Canada

Planning a horse move between Canada's West Coast and Atlantic Canada? Nexa Horse Transport welcomes enquiries for trips along our published Langley, BC to Halifax, Nova Scotia corridor, in either direction. Pickup locations, travel dates and space are reviewed before any booking is confirmed.

Request a horse transport estimate

From the West Coast to Atlantic Canada

Our published route connects horse communities in British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, New Brunswick and Nova Scotia. Stops may include Langley, Calgary, Edmonton, Saskatoon, Regina, Winnipeg, Thunder Bay, Sudbury, Toronto, Ottawa, Montréal, Québec City, Fredericton and Halifax, depending on the trip.

Eastbound and westbound requests

Whether you are moving a horse east for a new home or returning west after a sale or competition, send us both stable addresses and your preferred date range. Route fit, capacity and rest planning will determine what we can offer.

Cross-country horse travel planning

Long-distance horse transport requires careful consideration of loading, horse size and temperament, water and rest arrangements, seasonal road conditions and access at both barns. Share any special handling or veterinary needs before the trip is quoted.

Transparent estimates, not automatic reservations

Our online form helps gather route and horse details for an individualized estimate. A quote request does not guarantee a departure, stall or confirmed reservation. We will review logistics with you before confirming a journey.
