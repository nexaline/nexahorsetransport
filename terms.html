2. Bookings, quotes, prices and schedules

  • A request sent through our website, by phone or by text is not a confirmed booking. A booking is confirmed only when we confirm it to you in writing, by email.
  • The instant price on our website is an automatic estimate only. Your price is the one in the written quote we email you.
  • A written quote is valid for 30 days. It is based on the information you give us: route, pickup and delivery addresses, number of horses, access and special needs. If any of that changes, the price may change, and we will tell you before loading.
  • Prices are in Canadian dollars, per horse, per kilometer from Canada, plus GST. A minimum charge of $300 applies per trip.

3. Payment in full before delivery

Full payment is a condition of every trip. We never hold a horse to secure payment, so the money must reach us before your horse is delivered.

  • Pay the full balance at least 24 hours before the estimated delivery time, or right away when we send the final invoice if that is later. The final invoice includes the transport price and any extra charges under Section 5.
  • A payment counts only when the funds are in our account and available to us. A receipt, a screenshot or a "payment sent" message is not payment.
  • Pay by a method listed on your invoice. Any bank, card or transfer fees are yours to pay.
  • We do not hold horses for payment. Your horse's welfare comes first, and we will never hold back food, water, rest or care because of a payment issue. Delivering your horse does not reduce or cancel what you owe.
  • Any amount not paid by delivery is overdue at once. It is a debt owed to Nexa Line Ltd. by you and the owner, each fully responsible.
  • Overdue amounts carry interest at 2% per month (26.82% per year), from the delivery date until paid.
  • If an overdue amount is not paid within 7 days after delivery, we may start legal action to collect it without further notice, including a claim in the courts of Alberta. You will also owe our reasonable collection costs, the legal costs the court allows, and interest. We may use a collection agency.
  • If you disagree with a charge, tell us in writing by email to nexahorsetransport@gmail.com before delivery, or within 7 days after the invoice if that is later. Pay the part you do not dispute on time.
  • A complaint or a claim under Section 9 does not delay or reduce payment. Pay the invoice in full; we deal with the claim separately.
  • We may refuse future bookings from anyone with an unpaid balance, or require full payment before loading.

4. No deposit and free cancellation before loading

  • No deposit is required to book. The only exception is a client with an unpaid balance from an earlier trip (Section 3).
  • You may cancel free of charge at any time before your horse is loaded, even with less than 72 hours' notice. We kindly ask for more than 72 hours' notice when possible, so we can plan fairly for other clients. This is a request, not a condition.
  • If your horse is sick, injured, may have been exposed to a contagious illness, or is not ready to travel, please tell us right away. You can cancel or reschedule with no fee. A safe decision is always better than a risky trip.
  • If we arrive and your horse cannot be loaded safely, the trip is cancelled at no charge. Waiting or loading time beyond the included 30 minutes is still charged (Section 5).
  • We may cancel or postpone a trip before loading for safety, weather, road or vehicle reasons. We will offer a new date or refund any payment made for the service not provided. We are not responsible for any other costs the change causes.
  • Once your horse is loaded, the trip has started. If you then cancel or change the destination, you pay for the distance already travelled, any return trip, and third-party costs already incurred.

5. Extra charges

Your quote covers transport and normal handling under the conditions you told us about. We tell you about any extra charge as soon as we know of it.

  • Waiting and loading time: the first 30 minutes of loading or waiting at pickup are included. After that, difficult loading, a horse that is not ready, or no one available to receive the horse at delivery costs $100 for each extra 30 minutes. We never charge for delays we cause.
  • Access and distance: a difficult or unsafe access road, a location farther than you told us, extra stops you ask for, or a necessary detour may add a reasonable charge.
  • Surcharges: a fuel or detour surcharge may be needed if costs or route conditions change unusually. We explain it before charging whenever possible. If you do not agree to it before loading, you may cancel free of charge.
  • Third-party costs, charged at cost: veterinary fees, government charges, customs broker fees, border inspection fees and required permits, unless your written quote includes them.
  • Overnight layovers: for a trip entirely within Canada, Nexa pays the standard cost of an unexpected, necessary overnight layover that we arrange. This does not cover quarantine, detention or holding ordered by government or border authorities, border facilities, veterinary treatment, or medical boarding. On trips crossing the Canada–US border, quarantine, government-ordered holding and related third-party costs are yours unless we agree otherwise in writing (an email is accepted).

6. Health, fitness and honest disclosure

  • You must make sure each horse is fit for the planned trip, and give us complete and accurate information before pickup.
  • Tell us about any sickness, fever, coughing, nasal discharge, injury, lameness, pregnancy, recent disease exposure, medication or special care needs.
  • Tell us about kicking, biting, difficult loading, fear of trailers, or any other behavior that could put horses or people at risk.
  • If you are unsure whether your horse can travel safely, speak to a veterinarian before pickup and let us know. We may ask for vaccination records, health records or a recent veterinary check.
  • We will not load a horse that appears unfit or compromised, as federal humane transport rules require (Health of Animals Regulations, Part XII). Our driver's decision on loading is final.
  • If information you did not give us affects the trip, you are responsible for the costs and losses that result (Section 9).
  • Please be honest with us. We will never penalize you for cancelling before loading because your horse is unwell or not ready.

7. Loading, handling and care on the road

  • Have each horse and a capable handler ready at the agreed time and place. Each horse needs a properly fitted flat halter (rope halter is not acceptable) and lead rope. Travel boots, wraps and blankets are your choice.
  • Tell us about access limits, such as narrow roads, low wires or soft ground, and provide a reasonably safe area for loading and unloading.
  • We may decline or stop loading if a road, a facility, a horse or a person creates a safety risk. We may refuse service if there are threats, harassment, abuse or hostile behavior.
  • We take photos of each horse at loading and at delivery as a record of its condition.
  • On the road we make regular rest stops, provide hay and fresh water around the clock, and check the horses, in line with federal animal transport rules. We don't feed grain on the road. Please send enough bales of hay for the whole trip, plus any special feed, supplements or medication with clear written instructions.
  • You'll get at least 2 hours' notice before pickup and before delivery, as well as daily updates along the way.
  • For safety or the horses' welfare, we may change the route, stop, rest, call a veterinarian or adjust the schedule. We will contact you as soon as we reasonably can.
  • If a horse directly causes unusual damage to our trailer or equipment, beyond normal wear and tear, you are responsible for the reasonable, documented repair cost. Where practical, we will show you the damage before unloading. We never charge for damage that was already there or that we caused.

8. Emergency veterinary care and horse insurance

  • Give us a working emergency contact who can make decisions if you cannot be reached.
  • If your horse becomes ill or injured, we will try to contact you right away. If we cannot reach you or your emergency contact in time, you authorize us to call a licensed veterinarian and arrange the care the veterinarian considers necessary, including any steps animal welfare law requires.
  • Veterinarians are independent professionals. You pay reasonable veterinary and related costs, such as medicine and medical boarding. Nexa does not guarantee any medical outcome.
  • Nexa does not provide mortality, injury, illness or veterinary medical insurance for your horse. Our vehicle or carrier insurance is not a substitute for equine insurance.
  • You decide whether to insure your horse. We strongly recommend equine mortality and medical insurance that covers the whole trip, including loading, transport, layovers and unloading. Without it, your horse may be uninsured for some or all losses.

  • You are responsible for valid ownership information and every document the route needs. This may include brand inspection, a valid Equine Infectious Anemia (Coggins) test, health certificates, and import or export permits.
  • For travel between Canada and the US, documents must meet the requirements of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA), the US Department of Agriculture (USDA) and other border authorities.
  • You are responsible for extra costs, delays, refused entry, quarantine or other results of missing, incorrect or expired documents, unless Nexa caused the problem. Border and government authorities control their own inspections, decisions and timelines, and we cannot promise an outcome or a crossing time.
  • Arrange for a responsible person to receive your horse at the agreed delivery place and time, and keep your phone available for updates. The full balance must already be paid (Section 3).
  • At delivery, the person receiving the horse confirms delivery and the horse's condition by signature, text or email. If no one confirms, our delivery photos and trip records are the record of delivery.
  • If no one is there to receive the horse, or the destination is unsafe, we may board the horse nearby for its welfare, at your cost. We will try to contact you first. This is for the horse's safety, not to hold it for payment.
  • Belongings: one small tote or box, one saddle and one blanket travel free with each horse, if we have room and they are safely packed. Blankets must be packed in a tote or bag. Tell us in advance about any larger or extra items.
  • We take reasonable care with belongings, but to the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for lost or damaged tack, equipment or personal items. Please do not send valuables or irreplaceable items.
