Langley, our west coast hub

The Township of Langley is known as the horse capital of British Columbia, with Thunderbird Show Park and many breeding, training and boarding barns. Langley is the western end of our coast-to-coast route, which runs every two weeks to Halifax, Nova Scotia.

Langley

  • Campbell Valley, Brookswood and Milner: boarding barns and acreages
  • Thunderbird Show Park: show jumping and hunter competitions
  • Aldergrove, Fort Langley and Murrayville

Fraser Valley

  • Abbotsford and Chilliwack: along Highway 1 east
  • Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows: north of the Fraser River
  • Surrey and Cloverdale

Langley to Alberta

  • Highway 1, the Coquihalla (Highway 5) and Kamloops
  • Revelstoke, Golden and Banff to Calgary and Cochrane

Quick answers

How much does it cost to ship a horse from Langley to Calgary?

About 950 km. For one horse in a standard stall that is about $950 + GST ($1.00 per km). A large standing stall is $1.30 per km and the box stall $1.80 per km.

Does Nexa go to Langley regularly?

Yes. Langley is the western end of our coast-to-coast route, which runs every two weeks to Halifax.

Can you haul from Langley to Ontario or the Maritimes?

Yes. Horses from Langley can join our coast-to-coast run east, staying on Canadian highways.
