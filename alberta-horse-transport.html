Horse transport in Alberta

Based in Sundre, Nexa Horse Transport helps horse owners plan local and regional moves across Alberta. Whether the journey is to a new barn, a show, a trainer or a veterinary appointment, the same careful loading and route planning matter.

Request a horse transport estimate

Serving Alberta horse communities

Our Alberta service area includes Sundre, Calgary, Edmonton, Red Deer and communities along the routes between them. Pickups and deliveries outside major centres can be considered, too; send both addresses so we can check the driving route and schedule.

Barn moves, shows and veterinary travel

Shorter trips can still involve timing, loading and handling requirements. Tell us about your horse’s experience with trailers, the loading facilities at each end, and any special needs. For events, share the desired arrival window so timing can be discussed before booking.

A realistic quote before you commit

The request form calculates a preliminary estimate using the route and the horses travelling. We review logistics and availability before any transport is confirmed. A quote may change if pickup access, timing or the horse’s needs require a different plan.

Planning your Alberta pickup

Provide the full pickup and delivery locations, number of horses, ideal dates and any constraints on trailer access. Our team can then assess whether a local trip or a place on a wider route is suitable.
