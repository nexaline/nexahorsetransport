Horse transport in Prince Edward Island

Nexa Horse Transport brings horses on and off the Island over the Confederation Bridge, to and from Charlottetown, Summerside and farms across PEI, connected to our coast-to-coast run.

Our routes in Prince Edward Island

Every trip on or off the Island crosses the Confederation Bridge, between Cape Jourimain, New Brunswick and Borden-Carleton, PEI. From there we haul barn to barn across all three counties.

Queens County

  • Charlottetown, Stratford and Cornwall: Red Shores at the Charlottetown Driving Park, training barns, boarding
  • Hunter River and North Rustico: farms, trails and riding stables

Prince County

  • Summerside and Kensington: Red Shores at the Summerside Raceway, Standardbred barns
  • Borden-Carleton and O'Leary: bridge landing and western farms

Kings County

  • Montague and Pinette: training tracks and family farms
  • Souris: toward the eastern end of the Island

Transport routes

Bridge to Charlottetown

Moncton → Cape Jourimain → Confederation Bridge → Borden-Carleton → Charlottetown

Highway 16 to the bridge, then Route 1 east · about 170 km from Moncton

Bridge to Summerside

Borden-Carleton → Kensington → Summerside

Routes 1A and 2 · about 30–40 km from the bridge

Halifax to the Island

Halifax → Truro → Amherst → Cape Jourimain → Charlottetown

Highways 102 and 104, then Highway 16 and the bridge · about 270 km

Good to know

Bridge wind limits

The Confederation Bridge can stop vehicles towing trailers during high winds, mostly from October to March. We watch the bridge forecast and plan around it.

Barn to barn

Racetracks, training barns and farms anywhere on the Island. Send us the exact addresses.

Coast to coast

PEI trips connect to our run across Canada, every two weeks, so horses can move between the Island and the West.

Preparing for transport

Tell us the number of horses, their size, loading experience and any vet or handling needs.
