Our Services

Whether it's across town or across the border, we'll get your horse there safely.

Local & Regional Hauls

Trips around Sundre, Central Alberta and the rest of the province, including vet appointments, farm moves and trainer drop-offs.

Long-Distance Canada

Coast-to-coast transport across Canada, with planned rest stops, regular checks, and water and hay throughout the trip.

Canada – US Transport

Cross-border trips to and from the United States. We help you plan the health papers and border documents your horse needs.

Shows, Events & Sales

Reliable delivery to competitions, clinics, rodeos and sales, so your horse arrives on time and ready.

New Purchases

Bought a horse from out of province or out of country? We'll pick it up and bring it home.

Emergency & Vet Trips

Ask us about short-notice transport to veterinary clinics, subject to availability.
