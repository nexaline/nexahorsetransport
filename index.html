      Questions owners ask

      How do you care for my horse on long trips?

      Regular rest stops and fresh water around the clock. We don't feed grain on the road, and we ask you to send one bale of your horse's own hay, so there's one less change on a trip that already has a lot going on. You'll get at least 2 hours' notice before pickup and before delivery, and updates along the way.

      What is included in the price?

      Prices are in Canadian dollars, per horse, per kilometre, plus GST, with a minimum charge of $300 per trip. Vet, border, government and customs broker fees are extra and are charged at cost. Additional costs caused by delays at pickup or delivery, extra stops, layovers or border inspections may be charged. The online Booking / Estimate is an estimate only; nothing is confirmed until we call or email you.

      What papers does my horse need?

      Depending on the trip, your horse may need ownership papers or a brand inspection, a valid Equine Infectious Anemia (Coggins) test, health certificates, and any import or export permits. For travel between Canada and the US, documents must meet the requirements of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA) and the US Department of Agriculture (USDA). We may also ask for proof of vaccinations or a recent veterinary check.

      How do cancellations work?

      Please give us as much notice as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations made less than 72 hours before scheduled pickup may forfeit the deposit. If we have to cancel or postpone for safety reasons, such as severe weather or road closures, we will reschedule or refund any deposit paid. See our Terms & Conditions for the details.

      Can I get updates while my horse is on the road?

      Yes. If you agree to text messages on the Booking / Estimate form, we send pickup and delivery updates for your trip by text (message frequency varies; reply STOP to opt out). If your horse becomes ill or injured during the trip, we will try to contact you right away.
