Cumberland and Colchester

  • Amherst and Oxford: entry from New Brunswick, layover and boarding barns
  • Truro, Bible Hill and Brookfield: harness racing, exhibition grounds, boarding

Halifax and East Hants

  • Elmsdale, Enfield and Shubenacadie: hunter/jumper, dressage and lessons
  • Sackville, Waverley and Cole Harbour: boarding stables and riding academies

Annapolis Valley

  • Windsor, Falmouth and Wolfville: show barns, breeding farms, trails
  • Kentville, Berwick and Middleton: Quarter Horse and western training

South Shore and Pictou County

  • Bridgewater and Lunenburg: boarding, dressage, fairgrounds
  • New Glasgow and Stellarton: toward Cape Breton
